Word Crossy is a word puzzle that mixes letter-swiping with crossword grids. Swipe across the letters in the wheel to spell a word, and watch it drop into place on the board. Fill every blank to clear the level and move on to the next.

HOW IT PLAYS
• Swipe across the letters in the wheel to form words
• Each correct word fills its spot in the crossword grid
• Find every word on the board to win the level
• Shuffle the letters any time to see them from a new angle

STUCK? USE A HINT
• A hint reveals one letter on the board
• Hints cost rubies, and you never have to use them to finish a level
• Earn rubies by winning levels for the first time

EXTRA WORDS
Some words you swipe will not be on the board, but they may still be valid. Find these extra words, then claim a ruby reward. Each extra word counts once per level.

DAILY CHALLENGE
Come back every day for a fresh puzzle. Solve it to earn a reward and build a daily streak, with bonuses at streak milestones.

WORLDS TO EXPLORE
Pick a world, then pick a level. Swipe between worlds to see where you can play next.

IN-APP PURCHASES AND ADS
Word Crossy is free to play with ads. Optional in-app purchases are available, including ruby packs and an option to remove ads. Some rewards are earned by watching a video ad, which is always your choice.
