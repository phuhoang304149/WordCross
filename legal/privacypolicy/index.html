Word Crossy Privacy Policy

CatGaming · com.PHltd.wordcrossy · Last updated: October 30, 2026 (2026-10-30)

About this policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026. This policy explains how Word Crossy for Android (package com.PHltd.wordcrossy), published by CatGaming, handles information. Word Crossy is a free word puzzle game intended for people aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13. For privacy questions or data requests, contact support@catgaming.asia. Website: https://catgaming.asia.

No account required

You do not need to create an account or sign in to play. The game does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or precise location. Word Crossy does not operate its own user account system or its own game server for player data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, in-game coins and items, settings, and the state of your purchases (for example whether ads have been removed) in local app storage on your device. This information stays on your device; CatGaming does not receive it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage in Android settings removes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Crossy uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show ads. According to Google, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares information such as your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), advertising and other device identifiers, user interactions with ads and products, and diagnostic information about the performance of the app and the SDK. Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and ads may be personalized where supported and permitted. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by CatGaming. See Google Mobile Ads data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent and advertising choices

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent where required by law, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and the form shown depends on how the consent message is configured in AdMob. You can reset or limit the advertising ID at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads), and you can manage Google ad personalization at My Ad Center.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases (for example coins or removing ads) are processed by Google Play and its billing services using the Unity In-App Purchasing library. Payment details are handled by Google Play; CatGaming does not receive your card or payment details. Purchases are confirmed and restored through Google Play and applied on your device. The game does not send purchase receipts to a CatGaming server. See Google Privacy Policy and Unity Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game can show local reminders scheduled on your device. These are not push messages from a server and the game does not register for push notifications.

Updates to game content

The game may download game content and updates from CatGaming's content servers (data.catgaming.asia). Like any internet request, this download exposes your IP address and basic request information to the hosting provider.

Sharing

The game itself sends information only to the service providers described above: Google (advertising and purchases) and the hosting provider for content downloads. CatGaming does not receive the advertising data collected by Google.

Retention and deletion

CatGaming does not hold player accounts or player profiles, so there is no account to delete. Local game data can be deleted by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Information held by Google (for example advertising data or Google Play purchase history) is governed by Google's policies and settings, and requests about it should be made to Google through your Google Account.

Children

Word Crossy is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact support@catgaming.asia.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
